About the project

An AI product, not just a model

PropMind combines data analysis, machine learning, generative AI and retrieval into one real-world real estate platform.

Data

Ames, Iowa housing dataset

1,460 home sales (2006-2010) with 79 features from Kaggle's House Prices competition. After cleaning and removing two outliers, 1,458 homes with no missing values are used for analysis and modelling.

Machine learning

Random Forest price model

Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest were compared on a held-out test set. The Random Forest has the lowest average error and predicts from nine simple inputs.

Recommendations

Explainable matching

Homes are scored on budget fit, value against the AI fair-value estimate, quality and your preferences, and every result lists the reasons behind it.

Generative AI + RAG

Grounded answers

A prompt-engineered Groq LLM powers the advisor. The RAG assistant retrieves passages from four property documents with TF-IDF search and answers with citations.
